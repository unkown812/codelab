\subsection{Known limitations}
The playground editor itself lives in a cross-origin iframe and contributes no crawlable text; surrounding pages carry the indexable content instead. The social/OG image reuses the 261px favicon (no dedicated 1200px share image). \textbf{Status:} IMPLEMENTED (limitation) / RECOMMENDED (dedicated OG image, see Section~\ref{sec:roadmap}).
