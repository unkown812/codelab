\section{Design System}
\label{sec:design-system}

\textbf{Status:} IMPLEMENTED. Tokens are defined in \texttt{DESIGN.md} (Sanity-inspired) and implemented as Tailwind v4 \texttt{@theme} tokens in \texttt{src/app/globals.css}. Values below verified against both files on 2026-10-11.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Actual design values in use}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{8.5cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Token} & \textbf{Value and location} \\ \hline
Canvas & \texttt{\#0b0b0b} dark sections (\texttt{bg-canvas}); \texttt{\#ffffff} light commercial sections; \texttt{\#ededed} paper bands \\ \hline
Accent & Coral-red \texttt{\#f36458} (\texttt{bg-brand}), reserved for one primary action per viewport + brand dot beside the wordmark \\ \hline
Text & White on dark; ink \texttt{\#0b0b0b} on light; ash \texttt{\#b9b9b9} body copy on dark; mute \texttt{\#797979} eyebrows \\ \hline
Typography & Inter (display/body, tight negative tracking) + IBM Plex Mono (eyebrows/labels only) via \texttt{next/font}; display 112px $\rightarrow$ 38px scale \\ \hline
Radius & Pill \texttt{99999px} marketing CTAs; 3--6px \texttt{rounded-app-*} for inputs and studio windows; 12px \texttt{rounded-marketing} cards \\ \hline
Icons & \texttt{lucide-react} only (\texttt{ArrowRight}/\texttt{ArrowDown}), \texttt{aria-hidden}, sized to surrounding text \\ \hline
Section rhythm & 96px verticals; 1640px max container; dark/light polarity flips as dividers \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Editor chrome (framed screenshots, traffic-light dots, status pill) follows the same tokens with native-app radii so embedded product UI reads as product, not marketing.
